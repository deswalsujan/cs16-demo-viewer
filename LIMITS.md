# Known limits

What the viewer can't do yet, or does only roughly, in the current version (`VERSION`). Moved here word for word from the top of CHANGELOG.md on 30 Oct 2026 (Sujan's decision); every release updates it (AGENTS.md, "How each change ships"). Release entries in CHANGELOG.md still call this list "Known limits".

- The 3D view runs 0.1 s behind the kill feed, timers, sounds and radar, as the game's demo player seems to draw players (0.24.0; matched on Dosia's kill on cArn, fnatic vs Moscow Five, Nuke, DreamHack Summer 2011, not confirmed in the 1.6 demo player's code). The wallbang finder and the picture check still judge at the killing shot. The damage numbers, the ✕ and **Glow on hits** come with the kill feed since 0.26.3, beside the player as the 3D view draws him.
- The game's demo player draws players about 0.1 s back, so at the kill feed a victim can show a sliver that the wallbang finder, judging at the shot, doesn't see: THREAT on LoordB (FX vs Lions, Nuke, DreamHack Winter 2010, R22 0:47) reads strict where Sujan sees his elbow (Sujan, 1 Nov 2026: the finder keeps judging at the shot).
- Wallbang damage is worked out from the demo's health readings and hit sounds, since HLTV demos carry no "who hit whom" (0.24.0). The killing bullet shows only the health the victim had left. Each hit goes to the enemy who fired just before it with his crosshair closest; demos that lose fire events (most of 2008 and 2009) fall back on who was aiming at him, and a hit with two enemies about as close is left out. A bullet through another player loses damage like one through a wall, so a hit with a player on the line never makes a loose wallbang.
- A loose wallbang found from damage needs one of the killer's bullets through a surface 0.5 s or less before the kill (0.24.0; a millisecond more since 0.26.1, since the demo's times are rounded). A kill whose only bullet through cover came earlier stays a normal kill.
- HLTV demos don't record the first-person weapon's animation, so it's rebuilt from shots, weapon switches and the player's body animation. Timing can differ slightly from in-game, and idle variations won't match. If a `v_` model is missing, no gun is shown.
- Player models get even lighting, so they don't darken in shaded spots the way they do in-game.
- Custom models are named in the load summary but not checked further yet. One with an unusual skeleton may pose oddly; compressed or 24-bit sounds stay silent.
- A demo that's cut off, never finished or damaged partway plays up to where its readable frames end (0.16.0). Anything after a damaged stretch is left out, even if readable frames follow it. A file damaged in its first frames, before the match begins, still can't be played.
- Which rounds count is exact when the server's admin plugin announces the start ("Live !", "lo3", "3 restart and go !") and the end of each half in chat. Plugins word these differently, and a wording the viewer hasn't met yet falls back to a guess from the restarts, which can still count a warmup or miss a round. A file holding only a warmup, opened on its own, can show one round.
- HLTV demos store about ten snapshots a second, so everything between two snapshots is an estimate. A flick that starts and ends between snapshots can't be recovered, and a sharp turn can look slightly rounder than it was. The view always passes exactly through every recorded snapshot.
- At the moment of a kill, the shooter's crosshair can sit slightly off the victim, usually under 1.5° (through an AWP's 1x zoom that's up to about 60 pixels on a laptop screen). This comes from the demo, not the viewer: see 0.10.0, "Discussed, not changed".
- The sniper scope is rebuilt from the zoom click sounds, since HLTV demos don't record zoom (see 0.10.0). A click the recorder didn't hear would put the zoom one step off until the player's next weapon switch, death or round. Clicks are timed to about a tenth of a second.
- Theatre mode switches on by itself only with the viewer's own full screen (F or the Full screen button). The browser's full screen (F11 on Windows, the green window button or Ctrl+Cmd+F on a Mac) doesn't tell the page, so press T there.
- "Quality: auto" lowers 3D sharpness at most once per visit and doesn't raise it again on its own. Pick "Quality: high" to go back.
- Demos recorded by a player (POV demos) play, with two limits: the player's game only receives the players near them, so others drop in and out of view, and the wallbang finder is less reliable there than in HLTV demos. A recording that switched maps plays the map it spent longest on. With **POV mode** off, the default until Sujan signs it off, they play like HLTV demos and the recording player stands at the map's zero point, since the server never puts his own position in the snapshots; with it on, they play through his eyes (0.25.0).
- **POV mode** (0.25.0) finds no wallbang damage, and so no loose wallbang from damage: the server sends a player only his teammates' health. His crosshair's colour and size come from `cstrike/config.cfg` in the picked folder; without it, the game's defaults (green, medium). The other players are drawn 0.1 s behind his own view, as in the HLTV viewer: Sujan's decision of 29 Oct 2026, since neither 0 nor 0.1 s lined up clearly better at his kills. His aim for the wallbang finder is the snapshots' aim, as for everyone else, not the exact aim his own frames hold.
- Keeping the Half-Life folder between visits works in Chrome and Edge (122 or later) on an https page such as GitHub Pages. Firefox and Safari don't have the browser feature it needs, and the claude.ai copy is an embedded page, where browsers don't allow it; those pick the folder on each visit. A copy of index.html opened from your own disk also keeps it (checked in Chrome by Sujan, 1 Oct 2026).
- On maps with a lot of scenery (de_tuscan above all) the demo has no grenade in flight and no gun on the floor: the old engine sends at most 256 objects per snapshot and the map's own objects fill nearly all of them. Smokes, HE explosions and flashbang pops still show where and when they went off (see 0.14.0), but there's no flight path to draw.
- A map split over several demo files is joined by hand (Join the parts, or Join with another demo… in the Rounds tab). The viewer suggests the order from the recording times in the file names, or from the rounds; with 3 or more parts and no times in the names it can't, so the parts are put in order with the arrows. A demo with no real Steam IDs (a LAN server where every player has "0") isn't offered as a part; it can still be joined by hand, with players matched by name.
- A round played between two recordings counts in the score from the game's scoreboard, but its kills are in no file, so player stats leave it out.
- Whether rounds after 16 count can't be read from the demo, so the match stops at 16 unless Count them is switched on for that demo.
- When neither the file name nor a shared clan tag names a team, the header says "Team 1" and "Team 2" (iFNG FX vs fnatic, `auto_ifng-...`). Click the name in the header to type it in; it's remembered for that demo. Left as it is for now (Sujan, 2 Oct 2026; parked in IDEAS.md).
- The demo search looks at file names only. A demo whose name doesn't say the teams or map (`auto_ifng-...`) is found by scrolling.
- The wallbang finder rules out a kill that needed more damage than any shot through a wall can do (0.15.5). It needs the killer's shots in the demo; around a jump in the recording it can't tell, and the kill stays as the walls alone say.
- Health and weapon under the names in 3D show at any distance (0.16.0). With many players far away, the labels can overlap.
- A smoke cloud is drawn as one light green ball for as long as the smoke puffs (about 21 seconds, less when the round restarts). The game's own puffs drift and thin out unevenly, which the viewer doesn't copy.
- Some HLTV recordings skip ahead: at a few moments every player jumps far between two snapshots while the demo's game clock runs on as if 0.1 s passed (FX vs Lions, Nuke, DreamHack Winter 2010: 7 times). What happened in between isn't in the file, so no viewer can show it. Since 0.19.0 the viewer marks each skip and tags a wallbang that lands on one UNSURE, since the finder can only judge it from where everyone stood after the skip. A skip is found from what it does to the game (players moving faster than anyone can, health lost with no hit), so one where nobody moved or got hurt can't be found; checked against the bomb's fuse on 47 demos, none was missed (0.20.0).
- The dropped bomb on the radar needs the bomb object in the demo, so on de_tuscan it's often missing (the 256-object limit above).
- Round announcements use Verdana, the game's font; where it isn't installed (most Linux machines) the browser picks a similar one.
- The round and C4 timers stand still while the server is paused (0.26.5), found where the server's own clock stands still while the demo's runs on. Playback itself still runs through a pause at the demo's pace: in AGAiN vs Na`Vi, Train, recorded 24 Oct 2011, R7, that is 40 s of everyone standing still. A pause shorter than half a second isn't counted.
- The C4 timer above the view counts down from a fixed 35 s, the fuse every tournament demo measured so far uses (0.21.0). A demo from a server with another fuse (the game's own default is 45 s) would read wrong; the time since the plant in the Kills and Wallbangs lists is right either way.
- The wallbang finder's last check draws the killer's view at the shot (0.22.0), so it needs the 3D engine and the victim's player model. Without them (no WebGL, a missing model) the finder's 15 points decide alone and every wallbang reads as strict. The Wallbangs list settles a few seconds after the map loads, while it says "Checking N from the shooter's eye…". The victim's pose at the shot is the viewer's estimate (HLTV doesn't record animation frames), so a sliver of a limb can be off by a few units. Strict and LOOSE are split at 50 pixels of the victim in view (0.23.0); no line fits every close call: NEO on ArcadioN (74) and gob b on Xizt (223) read LOOSE, where Sujan finds the victim hidden. A kill the finder's 15 points see, with the crosshair through cover, is drawn too and listed under 50 pixels: strict at 0, LOOSE from 1 (0.26.0). Fences, grates and invisible walls (render mode 4) are drawn with their holes open, as the game draws them (0.26.6): they stay cover, so a kill through one is a wallbang, LOOSE when the victim shows through the holes. 0 pixels isn't always hidden: REAL on alleN (Sweden vs Norway, Dust2, ASUS ENC 2010, R25 0:46) and Tentpole on zet (SK vs eSTRO, Dust2, WCG 2008, R1 1:35) read strict where Sujan saw part of the victim.
- A kill that lands on a skip across a round restart (HLTV left out the end of the round and the restart) is never listed as a wallbang: where everyone stood at the shot isn't in the file (0.22.0).
