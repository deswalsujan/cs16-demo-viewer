## What was built

- `src/ui/wallbangs.js`: `teamKill` and `listedWbs`, used wherever `M.wbs` is made (also `checkSeen` in `src/view3d.part.js`): a kill between two players on the same side at the kill stays a wallbang (`k.wb`) but isn't listed. In `computeHits`, after the loose wallbangs from damage: a strict wallbang becomes LOOSE (`loose: 'open'`, `open: { dmg, low, before }`) when the victim's last health drop with a hit sound 0.15 s or less before the kill came with the killer firing in the 0.2 s before it and was at least `openLow` for his gun, the distance and the hit sound. `openParts` (from `bodyPart`, which now uses it) gives the parts a hit's damage fits in the open; the hit's zone lines are the line to his middle (as before) and the lines to those parts (`PART_Z`), and it's in the open when the crosshair line or any zone line is clear.
- `src/ui/sidebar.js`: the TK tag on every teamkill in the Kills list (`TK_TIP`); `src/ui/skips.js`: the LOOSE tooltip for the new reason; `src/app.css`: `.wbtag.tk`.
- `tests/expected/auto-1003031508-de_dust2.json`: Edward-FoCuS on Gux, R11 0:52, loose (Sujan, 8 Nov 2026). `tests/demo_search_check.py`: 3 checks.
