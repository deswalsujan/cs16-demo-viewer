## Item 1: up to 5 demos picked at once

- `#fDemo` (**Open .dem from elsewhere**) and the Join card's `#jFileIn` (**A file from elsewhere…**) take several files. `openPicked` (`src/ui/join.js`): one opens with `loadDemoFile` as before; two to five open the Join card, sorted by the recording times in their names (`fileTime`; names without one after them, by name), then the card's own suggestion; more than 5 set the start screen's message line, or the line under the card's picker, and open or add nothing.
- The checks of a part added to the card (another map, fewer than 8 of the same players, the same recording twice) run on every picked file. With several picked at once from **Open .dem from elsewhere**, the part the others are checked against is the one most of the others pass against, the earliest on a tie; from the card, it stays the card's first part. Each refused part gets its own card, one after another (`JOIN.errs`, which replaced the single `JOIN.err`; up to 0.27.2 two refused at once showed only the last). **OK** after the last one goes back to the card, or closes it when fewer than 2 parts are left.
- `suggestOrder` tries every order only up to 8 parts (40,320 orders): two picks of 5 could make 10, 3.6 million orders, which would stop the page.

## Item 3: the warning for a part that starts from 0:0

A part that isn't the first, has rounds that count, doesn't start late (no rounds on its scoreboard at its first counted round, not partway into a round), and isn't read as carrying on the previous part's half (`carryFacts`), with rounds that count in the parts before it: "Part N starts from 0:0, but part 1 already played 30 rounds." (one earlier part with rounds) or "but parts 1, 2 and 3 already played 42 rounds." The number is each earlier part's own count of rounds that count (`live`, as its line on the card says), with the rounds no file holds after part 1 (`missT`, `missCT`, as `roundsBefore` counts them). In the warnings, always in full; the verdict line's mark is ! while one shows; **Join parts** stays on.

### A narrower rule, measured, not built

Not when the part starts at a half's end (15, 30 and every 3 after in overtime; 12 and 24 in MR12), with the teams on other sides from the part before (or the same at an overtime's start: 30, 36, 42 and so on), and its own rounds change sides only where the map's halves end, no stretch on one side runs across round 15, and none has more than 6 rounds after round 30 (an overtime's second half and the next one's first can be on one side). Each part's own stretches on one side, from a throwaway script on the branch:

| Order | Part | Rounds before it | Sides from the part before | Its stretches | Narrower rule warns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FX vs mTw, Nuke | 2 | 15 | other | 18, 6, 2 | no |
| fnatic vs mousesports, Forge | 2 | 15 | other | 12 | no |
| mTw vs Na`Vi, Tuscan | 2 | 30 | other | 3, 3 | no |
| mTw vs Na`Vi, Tuscan | 4 | 42 | other | 3, 3 | no |
| Na`Vi vs SK 16:09, then the copy | 2 | 30 | other | 15, 15 | yes |
| 16:09, copy, 17:24, 17:40 | 2 | 30 | other | 15, 15 | yes |
| 16:09, 17:24, copy, 17:40 | 3 | 36 | same | 15, 15 | yes |

FX vs mTw, Nuke stays on its sides at the start of overtime (18 = 15 + 3; then 6 = 3 + 3), where Tuscan's teams swap at each overtime's start (3, 3), so both kinds of overtime have to pass. A match restarted from 0:0 partway through a half (a crash, the whole half replayed) still warns, since it doesn't start at a half's end.
