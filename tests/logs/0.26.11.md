## The change

- `src/ui/folder.js`: **Choose folder** opens the ordinary folder pick (`pickFolder` clicks `fFolder`) in every browser; Chrome and Edge's folder access is gone (`showDirectoryPicker`, the kept handle, `hlScanHandle`, `hlScan`, `hlCheck`, `hlReconnect`, the `reconnect`, `refused`, `remembered`, `scanning` and `error` states). `hlOS()` reads `navigator.userAgentData.platform`, else `navigator.platform`, and the row shows the Windows path, or the Mac's with its tip, or both. The row's states are `none`, `wrong` and `ok`; `ok` shows "N demos found." with no dot and **Choose again** with its tooltip. Nothing about the folder is saved; on load the page deletes what 0.26.10 kept (`hl:handle`, `hl:input`, `hl:snap`, with the new `idb.del` in `src/ui/common.js`). `checkDemoList` runs whatever the browser, and `dropDemos` keeps the row's count in step when a demo is taken off.
- `src/template.html`, `src/app.css`: the paths block has its own lines (`hlWin`, `hlMac`, `hlTip`) and the upload line; `hlAlt`, `btnHlInput` and `btnHlRefresh` are gone, with their styles.
- `src/ui/after-load.js`: going back to the start screen runs `checkDemoList`. `src/ui/open.js`, `src/ui/map.js`, `src/ui/summary.js`, `src/view3d.part.js`: "Refresh" wording and buttons now say "Choose" and open the picker. `src/ui/clear.js`: no "forget the folder" or **Remove access** in the button and its message.
