## The change

- `src/ui/wallbangs.js`, `computeHits`: every health drop the hit finder pins on one shooter is kept (`M.hits`), with its time, damage, hit sound, distance, gun, and whether it went through cover (true when it goes into the wallbang damage's `cover` list, so the wallbang results are as before); each health drop of the live rounds keeps where it came from (a shooter, a grenade or a fall, the finder's own reasons; `M.drops`), and `M.hpBefore` is kept for the health before a moment. Nothing it adds goes into `cover`, `proven` or the bursts.
- `src/ui/wallbangs.js`: `killHits` (the killer's hits on the victim up to the kill, each within 2 s of the next, in the order of the demo's health readings, then the killing bullet with the health left), `bodyPart` (head from the hit sound; chest or arm, stomach or leg from the damage by `GUN_DMG`, `GUN_ARMOR` and the game's multipliers, within 30 units of the recorded distance either way, only when one part fits, marked *), `earlierDamage` ("Earlier:", oldest first, "source unknown" where the finder has no reason, and for health the readings don't account for).
- `src/ui/sidebar.js`, `killDetailsHtml`: the box under the picked kill in the Kills and Wallbangs lists; a line's click seeks to that bullet (0.03 s after it, so its number shows), pauses, and goes to the killer's eyes. In **POV mode**: "No hit details. A demo recorded by a player has only his teammates' health, not <victim>'s." for an enemy, "No hit details in POV mode." for one of his teammates or himself.
- `src/view3d.part.js`: `numberGroups` picks the numbers by **Damage numbers** (Wallbangs: the bursts, as before; Direct hits: hits in the open and the killing bullet of a kill that isn't a wallbang; Both: every hit and every kill's killing bullet; Off: none); direct hits drawn in `#f4f1ea`, through a wall `#ffb347`; the killing bullet's skull drawn from the Players tab's own path (`SKULL_D`, `src/ui/names.js`) in `#ff3b30`. **Vanilla** (`VAN.on`) stops the crosshair kill markers, the 3D kill ring, the death cam's red wash and its "killed by" banner (the name bar and "press X for the next player" stay). In **POV mode** the **Damage numbers** row is hidden with **Glow on hits** and **Crosshair**.
- `src/ui/radar.js`: no kill ring on the 2D radar with **Vanilla**. `src/ui/playback.js`, `pillClock`: the round pill says "C4" after the plant with **Vanilla** on.
- `src/template.html`, `src/ui/loop.js`, `src/ui/common.js`, `src/app.css`: the **Vanilla** row first in **View settings** with a line under it, **Damage numbers** before **Glow on hits**, both kept in this browser (`vanilla`, `dmgNumbers`); `setVanilla` saves what it turns off and brings it back.
