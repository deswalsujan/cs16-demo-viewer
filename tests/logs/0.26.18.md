## The change

- `src/ui/loop.js`, `frame`: `requestAnimationFrame(frame)` first, then each part through `framePart`, which catches an error and hands it to `frameError`: the playback step, the kill flashes and sounds, the radar following the picked player, `updateFeed`, `draw` (2D), `drawPlayhead`, `updateCentre`, the 3D view (`build3d` and `update3`), `updateHud`. `prevT = T` and the flashes' clean-up stay outside, so a failing sound or flash step can't make kills land twice.
