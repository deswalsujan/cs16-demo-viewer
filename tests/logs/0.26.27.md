## What was built

- `src/shotfx.js` (new, in the 3D view's script after `src/audio.js`):
  - The gun flash. The game's sprites (`muzzleflash1.spr` to 3) are read from the picked folder, every frame of each. A shoot animation's event (5001, 5011, 5021, 5031) gives the attachment, sprite and size, as Xash3D's `R_MuzzleFlash` reads it. It is drawn additive, one frame long, at a seeded frame and roll.
  - The gun flash's frame: playing forward, a shot falls in it when it comes since the last frame drawn; paused or after a seek, when it comes up to 0.02 s before the moment.
  - From behind (`fxThirdPerson`): the player model's `ref_shoot_<ext>` or `crouch_shoot_<ext>` animation for his gun (`P_EXT`, from ReGameDLL's `DefaultDeploy` calls), and for the Elites the one for the hand that fired. The position is the p_ gun model's attachment when it has any, else the player model's own.
  - In **Player's eyes** and **POV mode** (`fxViewFlash`, called from `drawViewModel`): the v_ model's shoot animation at that shot, at its attachment, in the gun's own scene. None when the shot was from a sniper rifle's scope.
  - Shells (`fxShells`, `fxShellsOf`, `fxShellPath`), for the player whose eyes the view is. Each fire event gives one, as cs16-client's events do in first person with `cl_righthand 1` (each gun's forward, up and right numbers, in `SHELL`). The AWP's, Scout's and M3's come from the server's Brass message, with the client's 9-unit offset to the right and its throw to the left.
  - A shell's flight is worked out once in 100 steps a second against the map's world (as `HUD_TempEntUpdate` moves temp entities): gravity, half for a shotgun shell; a bounce reflects the speed and halves it; on a floor while slow it stops and lies flat; gone after 2.5 s.
  - Landing sounds (`fxShellSounds`, played from `soundTick`): the first bounce at 200 units/s or more, then one in four, as `CL_TempEntPlaySound` plays them. `player/pl_shell1.wav` to 3 at 0.8 for rifle and pistol shells, `weapons/sshell1.wav` to 3 at 0.5 for shotgun shells, both scaled by the fall speed over 350.
  - Blood puffs (`fxBlood`), at every `D.hitPoints` entry with a size, as Xash3D's `R_BloodSprite` draws them. The puff is `bloodspray.spr`, all 10 frames in 0.25 s, at size/25 to size/35, tinted from palette index 248 to 250. The drops are size + 2 to 16 `blood.spr` sprites, thrown out, falling at half gravity and stopping where they hit, lasting 1 to 3 s, tinted from index 247 to 249. The palette is `gfx/palette.lmp`.
- `src/demo.js`: keeps the Brass message (`D.brass`), read as cs16-client's `MsgFunc_Brass` reads it. `src/ui/open.js`: a joined map carries them.
- `src/ui/folder.js`, `src/ui/common.js`: the picked folder's `sprites/muzzleflash1.spr` to 3, `blood.spr`, `bloodspray.spr` and `gfx/palette.lmp` (`files.fx`), `cstrike`'s before `valve`'s.
- `src/view3d.part.js`: `fxBegin` and `fxWorld` in each 3D frame, each in its own try and catch. `src/players3d.js`: `fxViewFlash` in `drawViewModel`. `src/audio.js`: the six shell sounds preloaded, and the landing sounds in `soundTick` (not at 4x speed or faster).
- `build.py`: `shotfx.js` after `audio.js`. `AGENTS.md`: its line in "Where the code is".
