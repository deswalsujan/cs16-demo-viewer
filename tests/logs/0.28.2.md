## The text at the screen's size

- Up to 0.28.1 every text piece was made at the game's 800x600 size and enlarged: at a whole scale by the nearest pixel (blocky), otherwise to the next whole scale and shrunk with smoothing (soft). Now each piece is made at the view's own scale (`hudText`, s = device pixels a unit, the 12.34 px and 11 px fonts times s, 1 unit between letters) and put on the layer one to one on whole device pixels (`hudBlit`); the layout stays in units. At s = 1 the pieces are exactly 0.28.1's.
- The smear is one unit wide at any s: the whole pixels at 0.75 and the last part-pixel at its share. A first try with the smear round(s) pixels wide weighed 0.91 to 1.11 of 0.28.1's ink depending on s (heavier at 1.5, lighter at 2.4). Ink of the whole text against 0.28.1's at the same size:

| s | bars ("Counter-Terrorists : 0", "SWEDEN Tentpole <3 Tezz (93)") | feed and chat ("POLSKA NEO", "Sweden leading 1-0 (dust2 16-12)") |
|---|---|---|
| 1.5 | 0.993, 0.988 | 0.954, 0.959 |
| 2 | 1.002, 0.992 | 0.960, 0.960 |
| 2.4 | 0.975, 0.970 | 0.938, 0.938 |
| 3.27 (full screen on the Mac) | 0.967, 0.961 | 0.939, 0.934 |

  Widths are the same to the third decimal at every s.
- The icons (the gun and headshot icons, the clock icon) and the bars' rectangles are drawn as before (Sujan: the icons as they are for now).
