# 0.27.7: the Glock's bursts, the POV radar dots, the scoreboard's spectators

PR 1 of 3 of the plan Sujan approved on 28 Nov 2026 (`tests/logs/kill-feed-spectating-2026-11-27.md`, "The build plan" and "Sujan's answers on the plan"), on `wip/glock-radar-spectators` from origin/main (0.27.6, #71 merged). Every run on the Mac, with `HALF_LIFE_DIR=~/code/cs16-data/Half-Life` and `DEMO_DIRS=~/code/cs16-data/test-demos`; main is origin/main's `index.html` (0.27.6).

## What changed

- `src/players3d.js`: `GLOCK_BURST` (0.12 s) and `glockBursts`; `prepAnim` marks each Glock shot with the start of the burst it's in (three rounds within 0.12 s, one player); `vmAnim` plays `shoot` from the burst's start for those, `shoot3` for every other Glock shot (only with a view model that has `shoot3`, so `v_glock18.mdl`, not the shield's); `povVmAnim` the same for his own shots, from `D.ownBurst`.
- `src/view3d.part.js`: `povShots` also counts the rounds each of his shots took (his clip's drop, 1 to 3) and returns his Glock's bursts (`D.ownBurst`); `drawPovRadar`'s marks at half size.
- `src/ui/playback.js` and `src/app.css`: the scoreboard's spectators one per line, under an orange line (`.tline.spec`).

Where the rule's 0.30 s comes from: ReGameDLL's `GLOCK18Fire` (code, not measured): single fire's cycle 0.2 s less 0.05, one shot a trigger press, so single shots at least 0.15 s apart; a burst's second and third shots on the next player frames (`FireRemaining`).
