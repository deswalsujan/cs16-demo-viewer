## What was built

- `src/demo.js`: the reader keeps where the server marked a bullet's hit on a player, as `hitPoints` (time, x, y, z, size): TE_BLOODSPRITE with its size, and TE_STREAK_SPLASH (a helmet headshot's sparks) with size -1. SK vs EYESports has 400, SK vs Na`Vi, Train, DreamHack Winter 2011 758.
- `src/ui/open.js`: a joined map carries them, its parts' times moved like the rest.
- `src/ui/wallbangs.js`: in `tooMuchDamage`, an AWP or Scout kill with no shot of the killer's recorded in the window counts one bullet (neither fires twice in 0.6 s: `PrimaryAttack` waits 1.45 s and 1.25 s, `wpn_awp.cpp` and `wpn_scout.cpp`), unless the recording skips within 0.2 s of the window, or the victim has a hit point within 40 units across and 48 up or down of him in the 0.6 s before the kill (`hitPointOn`). Other guns stay as before: with no shot recorded, the rule leaves the kill alone.
- `tests/demo_search_check.py`: 3 checks on SK vs EYESports.
