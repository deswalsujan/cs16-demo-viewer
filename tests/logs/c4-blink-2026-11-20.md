# How the game blinks the planted C4 (20 Nov 2026)

The review chat's findings of 20 Nov 2026, before the 3D batch's item 2 (IDEAS.md, **The 3D batch after wip/ui-simpler**), recorded on `wip/3d-batch`. Sujan's answer the same day (`DECISIONS.md`): in the game's demo player the planted C4's light pulses as the code says, bright every 2 seconds and fading slowly so it's always faintly on, and it doesn't blink faster near the end.

## What the server sends

- The blink is a temp entity the server sends, TE_GLOWSPRITE (type 23), every 2 s from 2 s after the plant: `sprites/ledglow.spr`, 5 units above the bomb, life byte 1 (0.1 s), scale byte 3 (0.3), brightness 255 (ReGameDLL `ggrenade.cpp`, C4Think).
- The beeps keep their own 1.4 s clock; their sound changes (c4_beep1 to 5), the light's pace doesn't. The icon that speeds up (AnnounceFlashInterval) is Condition Zero's, or ReGameDLL's `show_scenarioicon`.
- HLTV gets every one: ReHLDS's SV_ValidClientMulticast returns true for a proxy. A POV demo gets them only while the bomb is in the recorder's PVS.

## How the game draws it

Xash3D `cl_tent.c` (R_TempSprite) and `gl_sprite.c`; cs16-client `entity.cpp` (HUD_TempEntUpdate); all rebuilds of the game's code.

- `ledglow.spr` is 64x64, additive, 2 frames, only frame 0 drawn.
- It faces the camera, 0.3 x 64 = 19.2 units wide.
- Full brightness for 0.1 s, then fading in a straight line to nothing over 2 s (fadeSpeed 0.5).
- Drawn with no depth test, but skipped when the line from the eye hits the map more than 8 units before the sprite; players don't hide it.

## The bomb entity

- `models/w_c4.mdl`. BombDrop gives the planter's origin; the bomb falls and lands about 0.2 s later, 17 to 22 units lower (Train's first plant, 10:23 into the demo: -341.9 at the plant, -359.9 on the floor, first blink at -354.9).
- The viewer's orange ball (0.27.1) sits at BombDrop's spot + 4, about 20 units in the air.
- Angles 0 0 0 at every plant in both demos (Valve's server; ReGameDLL's fix turns the bomb to the planter's yaw).
- A defuse removes the entity (DefuseBombEnd, UTIL_Remove) and the explosion hides it; the viewer's ball (0.27.1) stays until the round ends.
