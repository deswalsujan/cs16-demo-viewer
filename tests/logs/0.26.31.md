## The light tables

`buildCurve` in `src/light.js` against Xash3D's own test tables for BuildGammaTable (checked in a scratch script; the tables are Xash3D's and stay out of this repo):

| gamma, brightness, texgamma, lightgamma | texgamma table | light table |
|---|---|---|
| 2.5, 0, 2, 2.5 | 256 of 256 the same | 1024 of 1024 the same |
| 2.2, 1, 2.2, 2.4 | 256 of 256 the same | 1024 of 1024 the same |

The viewer uses 2.5, 1, 2, 2.5 (Valve's defaults). The models' shader works the light table out itself (no texture in the vertex shader): in single precision it gives the table's value at all 1023 entries.

Nuke and Dust2 matched only once the "angle" key was read as the engine reads it (ED_ParseEdict: the yaw, keeping the pitch already set). The colours differ by up to 2 where the server (Valve's mp.dll) works `r * (v / 255.0)` in single precision. The movevars in each frame's view block are empty in HLTV demos and, in POV demos, hold the last map's on a new map's first frames, so the reader keeps svc_newmovevars and falls back on the last frame's. Korfez's and Mirage's suns are level (pitch 0): their light_environment sets "angles" after "pitch", which puts the pitch back to 0, and the server sends it so.

## The light on models

As R_EntityDynamicLight, for the world only (Xash3D's `r_lighting_extended`, on by default, also reads brush entities and the ceiling; the game doesn't), each face's first light style at 264, no dynamic lights. On SK vs Na`Vi, Train (`tests/light_check.py`): starix in the dark corner at R4 1:30, 12 (1.2 all round, 10.8 from one direction); edzie in shade at R16 1:39, 57 (the mockup, reading the drawn mesh, said 55 for the byte, the engine's texel is 56); RobbaN in the open at R4 1:20, the sky's 363 363 239: 36.3 all round, 218.7 from the sun's direction. Delpan at R3 1:37, just inside a tunnel's mouth, is lit by the sun too: the line from him toward the sun meets the world's sky face 464 units out, through the opening (in the By-eye round, to check in the game).

Speed: 2 µs a model (one sky walk and five light walks) on de_train, de_dust2 and de_korfez32, 2,000 random points each.
