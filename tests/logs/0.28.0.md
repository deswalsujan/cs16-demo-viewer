PR 2 of 3 of the plan Sujan approved on 28 Nov 2026 (`tests/logs/kill-feed-spectating-2026-11-27.md`, "The build plan" and "Sujan's answers on the plan"; `DECISIONS.md`), on `wip/hud-kill-feed-bars` from origin/main (0.27.7, #72 merged). Every run on the Mac, with `HALF_LIFE_DIR=~/code/cs16-data/Half-Life` and `DEMO_DIRS=~/code/cs16-data/test-demos`; main is origin/main's `index.html` (0.27.7).

## What changed

- `src/hud.js` (new, in the 3D view's script; `build.py`, `AGENTS.md`): the game's 800x600 units (600 high, as wide as the view's shape) and their scale for a canvas (`hudScale`); each piece (a text, an icon, a sprite) drawn at the whole scale when the scale is whole, otherwise enlarged to the next whole scale with no smoothing, the copy kept, and shrunk to the view with smoothing (`hudUp`, `hudImg`, `hudRect`); the game's text (`hudMask`, `hudText`, `hudDrawText`): since Sujan's decision of 28 Nov 2026 the browser's own smoothed Verdana Regular, 12.34 px (EngineFont: the scheme's height 15 / 1.2153) or 11 px (Default), the line drawn at once, 1 px between letters, no edge cutting, a 0.75 smear, a 1-px black shadow for EngineFont; each text made once per colour (the first build cut every pixel at half; R6, hard-edged, was built and dropped the same day: `tests/logs/hud-text-variants-2026-11-28.md`); the kill feed's icons from the player's `hud.txt` and 640hud sheets at their own size, tinted 255 80 0 or 10 240 10 (`hudLoadIcons`); the clock icon from `gfx/vgui/800_timer.tga` at 90% (`hudLoadTimer`); the kill feed (`drawGameFeed`) and the spectator bars (`drawGameBars`, `hudBarsWanted`, `hudBarsClass`). `povBarsOn` moved here from `src/view3d.part.js`.
- `src/view3d.part.js`: `drawPovHud`, `drawPovBar`, `drawPovRadar` and `povRadarRoom` in the units (600 lines; `H / 768 * HUD_SCALE` up to 0.27.7), their sprites through `hudImg` (unsmoothed), the radar's dots on whole units; `update3Now` draws the bars and the feed; POV mode's own bars (`SPEC_BAR`, `drawSpecBars`) gone; `placePovNotices` reads the game feed's rows in 3D; `setView` and `resetForNewDemo` take the bars' room away.
- `src/ui/folder.js`: the folder pick reads `gfx/vgui/800_timer.tga`, cstrike's first, then Half-Life's (and `HL_WANT` has it).
- `src/ui/playback.js`: the round pill "Round N" alone while the bars are up.
- `src/app.css`: the viewer's own feed hidden in 3D; while the bars are up (`.bars`, `--bar-h`), no header score outside Theatre mode, the camera buttons under the top bar, the strip, help line, name banner, notices and corner radar above the bottom bar, POV mode's pill in the top bar.

## Choices made while building (not in the plan; Sujan to confirm or change)

1. Each piece is scaled on its own, its enlarged copy kept, rather than the whole layer drawn on one canvas at the next whole scale and shrunk: the pixels are the same where pieces don't overlap, with no full-view canvas at 2 to 5 times the view's size (a 27-inch screen at 2x would need one of about 3000 by 5300). "Redrawn only when what it shows changes": the letters, the costly part, are made only when a text changes (the clock once a second, a new kill); each frame copies the pieces already made, about 30 small copies.
2. A followed player who dies in an HLTV demo: the bars stay, his name in the bottom bar without health, and the viewer's banner saying who killed him stays too, lifted above the bottom bar (with **Vanilla**, which holds only the name there, it goes).
3. The bars' score is the match's, the header's numbers, each on its side's line. 0.27.7's POV bars took the score by team, so the Counter-Terrorists line showed the second team's score whichever side it played (fixed with it). The game's own TeamScore would restart at a half's restart.
4. "Spectators n" only when the HLTV demo carries the viewer count (it has no line otherwise); in a POV demo while he's dead, his money there ("$14950"), as in his YouTube POV.
5. In Theatre mode the header keeps its score: when it slides in, it covers the top bar's score.
6. In Theatre mode the side panel, when it slides in, covers the feed and the bars' right part (0.27.7's feed moved left of it): left as the game draws it, a question for Sujan (IDEAS.md, the item's "Open for Sujan").
7. Without the player's `d_` icon for a gun, the gun's name in orange where the icon goes.
