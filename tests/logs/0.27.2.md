## Item 1: the POV radar solid for everyone

cs16-client `cl_dll/hud/radar.cpp`, CHudRadar::Draw, lines 292 to 300: with cl_radartype 1, `SPR_Set(m_hRadarOpaque.spr, 200, 200, 200)` and `SPR_DrawHoles`; then the dots with kRenderTransAdd. hud.txt: `radaropaque 640 radaropaque640 0 0 128 128`. On the Mac, `radaropaque640.spr` and `radar640.spr` are both 17,214 bytes and differ from byte 13, the sprite's format.

The viewer: `radaropaque640.spr` tinted 200 200 200, palette colour 255 see-through, drawn normally on `#spec3` (over a flashbang's white, as the spectator bars); the dots stay on `#hud3`, added. Seen on the GP Tourney POV of Team Guardians vs Lanka Dahan, Mirage, 25 Oct 2026 (`forgotten-de_mirage-2026.dem`), R11, 25 s in, against 0.27.1: the solid green disc with its view lines in place of the faint see-through one, the same dots. `pov_feedback_check.py` looked for the radar's pixels on `#hud3`; it looks on `#spec3` now (a solid sprite there, `#spec3` blended normally) and passes, 28 s.

## Item 3: the sky

Read in Xash3D FWGS: `engine/client/dll_int/ref_common.c` (R_SetupSky, CheckSkybox: `gfx/env/<name>` with the sides rt, bk, lf, ft, up, dn, .dds then .tga then .bmp, with or without "_"; "desert" when the named sky is missing), `ref/common/ref_skybox.c` (r_skyTexOrder 0, 2, 1, 3, 4, 5) and `ref/gl/gl_warp.c` (R_DrawSkyBox and MakeSkyVec: each side's corners from st_to_vec, the picture's top row at t = 1, drawn unlit with GL_REPLACE). Texgamma goes only on Half-Life's own palette textures (`engine/common/imagelib/img_utils.c`, LUMP_TEXGAMMA, set in `img_wad.c`), so the sky pictures go in as they are.

The viewer, as Sujan asked: worldspawn's skyname, "desert" without one; the six pictures from cstrike, cstrike_downloads or valve (that order), .tga before .bmp; all six or none, and none keeps the grey-blue background. (The engine falls back to "desert" when a named sky is missing; Sujan's rule is the grey-blue.)
