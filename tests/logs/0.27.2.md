## Item 4: the light at gamma 3 and brightness 2

### First try (not committed): gamma 3, brightness 2, overbright kept

`buildCurve(3, 2, 2, 2.5)` in place of `buildCurve(2.5, 1, 2, 2.5)`, still one curve for everyone. Xash3D's BuildGammaTable (`engine/client/gamma.c`) multiplies the light by brightness before the curve's knee when brightness is above 1; `buildCurve` did, but the models' shader keeps its own copy of the curve, which left that step out (it never ran at brightness 1), so it gets it too. The shader's curve against the table: no entry more than 1 apart, of 1,024. The engine allows gamma 1.8 to 3 and brightness 0 to 3 (V_ValidateGammaCvars), so gamma 3 and brightness 2 are within them.

What it does to the numbers, Valve's defaults then gamma 3 and brightness 2: a lightmap byte drawn (`lm`, before the 4/3 blend) 12 -> 17 then 34, 56 -> 83 then 126, 128 -> 148 then 193, 255 -> 255 both; a texture byte through texgamma 64 -> 84 then 101, 128 -> 146 then 161.

On `screenshots/0.27.2`, **Player's eyes**, 1440x900, the same frame before (the branch with items 1, 2, 3 and 5) and after: SK vs Na`Vi, Train, DreamHack Winter 2011, face (slot 4) at R20 1:24 (demo 2405.77) and R21 1:15 (2519.16), starix's dark corner at R4 1:30 (slot 8, 801.96); Korfez in the sun, Sujan's POV (`s-deswal-korfez-knd.dem`), R9 1:09 (759.49), in **POV mode**; and all four side by side (`00-light-before-after.png`).

Sujan, 20 Nov 2026 (`DECISIONS.md`): his game on Windows at gamma 3 and brightness 2 has no overbright in either build: steam_legacy has `gl_overbright` 0; the 25th Anniversary build has `gl_use_shaders` 1 and `gl_overbright` 0, and both look the same. Measured in the review chat on his Korfez screenshots, sunlit sand at the bottom left: the game reads about red 220 to 250, green 198 to 232, blue 146 to 187, with visible texture (spread 5 to 28) and 3 to 60% of pixels at 250 or more; the viewer's R9 1:09 frame read 255 255 170 on main and 255 255 232 with the first try, 100% at 250 or more, no texture left. His six 25th Anniversary screenshots are on `screenshots/0.27.2` (`20-korfez-25th-1.jpg` to `25-korfez-25th-6.jpg`, from `~/Downloads/korfez-25th/plain-names`).

What gl_overbright changes, read in Xash3D FWGS (11ed246):
- The map's lightmaps (`ref/gl/gl_rsurf.c`, R_BuildLightMap): with `gl_overbright` 1, lightscale 256 (without VBO); with 0, `pow(2, 1 / lightgamma) * 256 + 0.5`, 338 at lightgamma 2.5, so the lightmap is built brighter to make up for the next point.
- How they're drawn (R_BlendLightmaps): with 1, `GL_DST_COLOR, GL_SRC_COLOR` at the colour 128/192, the viewer's 4/3; with 0, `GL_ZERO, GL_SRC_COLOR`, the texture times the lightmap, at most the texture.
- Fog's colour is halved with 1 while lightmaps blend (GL_SetupFogColor); the viewer draws no fog.
- Studio models: nothing. `ref/gl/gl_studio.c` never reads `gl_overbright`; R_StudioLighting looks a vertex's light up in the same light table either way (LightToTexGamma), and the light under a model comes from the map's own light samples (`ref/common/ref_light.c`), not from the built lightmap. The viewer's models (`STUDIO_VS`, `entityLight`) already work that way, so nothing else in it came from `gl_overbright` 1.

The viewer: `buildCurve(3, 2, 2, 2.5, false)`, the lightmap table at lightscale 338, and the map's shader without the 4/3. The lightmap's multiplier on the texture (of 255) at lightmap bytes 12, 56, 128, 200 and 255: main 23, 111, 197, 255, 255; the first try 45, 168, 255, 255, 255; no overbright 43, 141, 235, 255, 255. A fully lit surface is now its texture's colour (through texgamma at gamma 3), never more.
