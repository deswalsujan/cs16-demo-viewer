## 1. The scope's speed rule on all 88 test demos

The measure, as in the report: an AWP fire event whose spread reads exactly 0 is a shot fired scoped, standing or ducking (the demo stores the spread to 0.01; scoped it's at most 0.0015, unscoped 0.08 or more). For each such shot, whether the viewer shows him scoped: today's rule (main, 0.28.2) against this build. The fire event's spread was kept by a copy of the built page with three lines added to the reader, in a scratch folder.

**First run, the rule as first built** (the speed rule on every scope, clicked or come back by itself; slopes let through with the height test of section 3):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HLTV demos (79) | 3,092 | 2,727 | 2,727 | 17 | 17 |
| POV demos (9) | 5,826 | 905 | 907 | 5 | 7 |

The 17 HLTV losses: 10 in uncounted stretches (warm-ups and freeze times of Norway vs Romania, Tuscan, 12 Feb 2009; Sweden vs Poland, Dust2, 8 Apr 2009; FX vs eSahara, Dust2 and Nuke, DreamHack Winter 2010; EG vs eXecutors, Train, EM4 finals), where players ran at 200 units/s and more with a clicked scope; clicked scopes read at 168 to 190 units/s just after the click (Delpan, Sweden vs Norway, Dust2, ASUS ENC 2010, R6 1:13 and 1:11; starix, mTw vs Na`Vi, Tuscan, ESWC 2010, R1 1:16); position jumps of 974 to 1,488 units/s in the recording (IoaNN, Virtus.pro vs ALTERNATE aTTaX, Inferno, EM II, R20 1:46 and 1:44); and an earlier wrong close shifting the next clicks by a step (SpawN and edzie, Sweden vs Ukraine, Train, ClanBase NationsCup XI). So the build was narrowed:

**The rule as built**: only a scope that came back by itself after the bolt is cancelled (the quick-switch Sujan confirmed); a clicked scope stays; a half second with a step faster than 500 units/s is a jump in the recording and doesn't count.
