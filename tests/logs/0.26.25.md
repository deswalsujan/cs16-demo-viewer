## What was built

- `src/ui/lookups.js`: `teamsFromFileName` cuts a team's part of the file name to the clan tag all its players share (`sharedTags`, the start or the end of their names, cut back to a separator) when the file's part runs on past it, the tag read through the known teams; a known team's name in the file name stays as it was.
- `src/ui/skips.js`: in `findSkips`, at a round start, a kill in the respawn snapshot stands in for silent damage when only 1 or 2 players are alive; `onRestartSkip` for the snapshot of a skip across a restart.
- `src/ui/wallbangs.js`: in `computeHits`, health readings in the snapshot of a skip across a restart count in the round before it (`roundOf`), and a drop there gets no shooter; `earlierDamage` gives its time on that round's clock (`killClock`).
- `src/ui/sidebar.js`: kill details say "the recording skips here" for the killing bullet of a kill on a skip across a restart.
- `src/ui/playback.js`: `updateFeed` shows only kills from the round playing now (`roundAt`), within 6 s as before.
- `tests/demo_search_check.py`: 5 checks on SK vs AGAiN. `tests/expected/SK_vs_AGAiN_ArbaletEU-0912051316-de_train.json`: walle on Neo, R18 1:06, loose with a known gap (added with the report).
