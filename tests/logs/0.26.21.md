## The rule as approved, and why it changed while building

Built first as approved (the match starts at the first live round with both teams full), mousesports vs mTw, Inferno, IEM4, joined, read 14:10 in 24. Opened alone, part 2's first live round (16:13, after the server crashed and "Buffering game…") was played 2 against 5 for its whole length, mTw's players reconnecting: the rule dropped it, part 2's first counted round then showed a round already on the game's scoreboard, the join read a round played between the recordings, and part 1's seven rounds were judged replayed. That round is one of the 30 of the 19-11. Both teams had been full in earlier rounds of part 2 (the replayed ones); in compLexity vs WeMadeFox and SK vs fnatic, Train, they had not. So the rule became: rounds before the first round of the file with both teams full, ever, don't count. Players at 5 s, 20 s and the end of the rounds concerned: compLexity vs WeMadeFox R1 to R5: 1:1, 4:2, 5:3, 5:2, 5:4 at 5 s (the first full round at 8:48); SK vs fnatic, Train: 3:1 to 4:4 in the 15 rounds from 0:08 to 10:36, the first full round at 18:42.
