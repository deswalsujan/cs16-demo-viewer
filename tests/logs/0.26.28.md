## Crashes 1 and 2

One cause: `loadDemoFile` replaces `D` before `onDemo()` runs `povInit()`, and while the overview loads in between, frames ran with `POV.on` still set from the POV demo; a demo with no view of his (an HLTV demo, or the POV demo opened again with **POV mode** off) made `povView` throw "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'length')". On 0.26.16 that stopped the frame loop for good (the freeze Sujan saw); from 0.26.18 it logged the error and showed the error toast. Fix: `resetForNewDemo` clears POV mode's state, and `povInit` turns it on only for a demo with `D.view`.

`tests/pov_reopen_check.py` (Match 1 T in **POV mode**, playing in 3D; **POV mode** off; on; then mTw vs mousesports, Inferno, IEM4 World Championship, part 1; the overview's load held 0.3 s so frames always run in that gap): 0.26.27 fails all three steps after the first with that error; the branch passes, 16 s.

## The team split

Two faults in `derive()` (`src/ui/match.js`):

1. A pair's counts were kept under "a|b" or "b|a" by the order the two came in that round, which is their slots' order, so a pair whose slots swapped was counted under two keys. On the new demo, malaiiiiiiiiiiii moved from slot 31 to slot 16 after R1, and the pair with Yedzavni Shizuka read "same side 1, apart 0" under one key (R1, before the teams were set) where it is 1 and 22. Now one key per pair, the ids sorted.
2. Any one pair "together more often than not" joined their groups. On Match 2's CT file (public server, de_mirage_32, 27 Mar 2025), one player played 2 rounds on T, then 10 on CT; three players seen only in his 2 T rounds were "together 2, apart 0" with him, so T and CT became one team (all 41 in Team 1).

`groupTeams` (new): two groups join only when, over all the pairs between them, rounds on the same side outnumber rounds apart; the two with the biggest lead join first, a tie going to the larger same-side count, then to the groups whose first people came first in the demo. `tests/pov_teams_check.py` runs it on 20 shuffled orders of the players and of their pairs (each pair's key reversed at random): the same teams 20 of 20 on both demos.

## Rounds in POV demos

In a POV demo a round starts at the round timer only after the last round ended, and a restart ends no round, so each run of restarts read as one round, its sides sampled 5 s in, before the last restart. On the new demo: the 5 restarts after the config load (1:39 to 2:07) and the 5 at the half (26:58 to 27:23), so R2 and R17 had the sides of the wrong moment and gave a team a win on T and on CT in one half. The reader (`src/demo.js`) now also starts a round at the first round timer at least 0.5 s after a "#Game_will_restart_in". Rounds before and after, on all 8 POV test demos (node, the reader alone): every round with a result keeps its result; the restarts split off (the start of map's "lo3" on all 8 used to be one round with the first real one); on the new demo two admin restarts mid-half after a player dropped (10:34 and 23:34, the round replayed) split the round they happened in.

On the new demo it now reads, with **POV mode** on: BAJRANG and Guardians, 14 each; R3 to R17 Lanka Dahan on T (Guardians 12-3), R18 to R25 on CT (4-4): the 23 rounds from the config load give exactly 16-7.
