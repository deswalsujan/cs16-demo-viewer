## The change

- `src/view3d.part.js`: `eyeFov(fov, aspect)` gives three.js's field of view (top to bottom) from the game's (90, or a zoom level, as the width of a 4:3 picture): `2 * atan(tan(fov / 2) * max(0.75, 1 / aspect))`. Wider than 4:3 it keeps the 4:3 picture's height; narrower it keeps the fov across the width, as before. Used for **Player's eyes** (unzoomed and through a sniper scope), the death cam, and **POV mode** (his zoom from SetFOV, or the zoom of the player he spectates). The gun in hand already took the main camera's fov. Free view and **Behind player** keep their fixed 70 degrees top to bottom; the wallbang picture check keeps its own camera.
- `src/view3d.part.js`: `povXhW(W, H)`, `min(W, H * 4 / 3)`, is the width **POV mode**'s crosshair (`drawPovCrosshair`) and the kill marker's ticks (`povXh`) are sized from, line width included, in place of the view's width.
