## The change

The picture check (`checkSeen` in `src/view3d.part.js`) no longer swaps fences, grates and invisible walls (render mode 4 brushes, textures starting `{`) for flat black: they're drawn with their own texture, whose holes the 3D view's shader leaves open, as the game draws them. The finder's sight points (`computeWallbangs`) are unchanged, so these surfaces stay cover and a kill through one stays a wallbang.
