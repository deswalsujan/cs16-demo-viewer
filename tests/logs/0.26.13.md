## The change

- `src/app.css`: every size written `calc(Npx * var(--ui))` (210 of them, converted by a one-off script), except 1px lines, media query conditions and the round announcements' `--cm-size`; `--ui: 1` on `:root`.
- `src/ui/size.js` (new, in the page's head so the first paint already has the right size): `uiScaleFor` (Auto: `min(1.5, w / 1920, h / 1000)`; a picked size as it is; both capped at `min(w / 1280, h / 640)`, never below 1, rounded down to 5%), `applyUiSize` (sets `--ui` and `UI`), and listeners for `resize` and for a change of `devicePixelRatio` (a `matchMedia` resolution query), which send `uisize`.
- `src/ui/loop.js`: the **Interface size** list (`#ui3`, saved as `uiSize`); on `uisize` the radar and timeline canvases (`resize`) and the 3D view (`applyQuality`) are sized again, and Theatre mode's bars measured.
- Canvas labels at `UI`: `src/ui/radar.js` (names and HP line, dots, view cones, the picked player's ring, C4 tags, the planted bomb, death crosses, kill rings, the grenade's head, the click distance for picking a player); `src/ui/timeline.js` (the whole strip drawn `UI` times larger in a space `UI` times smaller, hover distances); `src/view3d.part.js` (names, HP and gun, damage numbers, HS and ✕, the kill marker's label, the kill ring, the spectator bars' text, the POV HUD's gap above the bottom buttons, the hidden-enemies note's style).
- Inline styles through `--ui`: the toast (`src/ui/after-load.js`), the kill feed icons' width (`src/ui/folder.js`); Theatre mode's edge zones times `UI` (`src/ui/theatre.js`).
- `src/template.html`: `ui/size.js` in the head; the **Interface size** row in **View settings**, with its tooltip; **View settings**' own tooltip names it.
