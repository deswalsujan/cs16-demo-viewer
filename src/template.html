Colours and marks

Kill markers

In Player's eyes, small ticks flash around the crosshair when the player you're watching gets a kill. In the other views, a ring in the same colour bursts out from the victim.

KILLA normal kill. Example: an AK spray to the body in the open.
HEADSHOTThe killing bullet hit the head. Example: a one-tap with the AK.
WALLBANG · HSThe bullet went through cover, with the victim fully hidden. HS is added when it was also a headshot.
PARTIAL WALLBANG · HSA partial wallbang: the bullet went through cover, but part of the victim was in view. HS is added when it was also a headshot.

The side panel

Players: kills and deaths come from the kill feed, so there are no bonus points for bomb plants or defuses, and a team kill (TK) isn't counted as a kill. Only the rounds that count are counted; overtime is every round after the regulation ones. Click a player to follow them; Key is the 1 to 0 key that jumps to them. In a POV demo the highlighted player is the one whose eyes the view shows.

Rounds: the score after each round, and how long it lasted. Show N uncounted rounds, at the top of the list, also lists the rounds that don't count: the warmup, restarts, idle rounds and rounds before the match.

The timeline

RoundsEach round's stretch, red when the Terrorists won it, blue when the Counter-Terrorists did, grey with no result; its number above it.
KillsA thin bar in the killer's team colour; a taller one for a headshot. Click one to watch that kill and open it in the Kills tab; in a POV demo, to jump to a few seconds before it.
DiamondsA wallbang (pink), a partial wallbang (lilac), and wall damage, hits through a wall with no kill (hollow orange).
TrianglesThe bomb planted.
Gaps// where the recording skips: HLTV left part of the game out, so players jump.
PausesA light stretch where the match was paused.
PlayheadWhere playback is. Click or drag on the timeline to move it.
