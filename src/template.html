Kill markers

In Player's eyes, the crosshair flashes when the player you're watching gets a kill. In every view, a ring in the same colour bursts out from the victim.

KILLA normal kill. Example: an AK spray to the body in the open.
HEADSHOTThe killing bullet hit the head. Example: a one-tap with the AK.
WALLBANG · HSThe victim was fully hidden and the bullet went through cover. HS is added when it was also a headshot.
